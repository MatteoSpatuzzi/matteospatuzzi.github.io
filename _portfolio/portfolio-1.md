"Participated in the organisation as well as an attendee iof the [4EU+ DeepLife 2024 course](https://deeplife4eu.github.io/) and Hackathon member during my Master Thesis Project. The [4EU+ circle](https://4euplus.eu/4EU-1.html) is a network of european universities inclusing Paris Sorbonne, Heidelberg University and Politecnico di Milano amongst others. The circle facilitates the exchange of ideas as well as academic cooperation between universities. DeepLife is a collaborative lecture series accompanied by pratical excersies in the development and deployment of deep learning models for life science data analysis. The lectures include an introduction to multi-layer perceptrons as well as variable autoencoders, convolutional networks such as U-networks, Transformer architectures, recurrent neural networks and more. 

The course was organised by [Prof. Dr. Carl Herrmann](https://www.hdsu.org/), who acted as host for the event at Heidelberg University.

I participated to the course's Hackathon in the project of protein function prediction based on protein sequence data."
