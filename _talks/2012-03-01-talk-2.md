Gave a introductory course to the world of omics to Educators across Europe to part of the [Cell Biology and Omics for Educators](https://www.embl.org/ells/training/cell-biology-and-omics-for-educators/) course. In my talk, I touched on the main modalities of omics data, single-cell and spatial modalities as well as the potential for integration of multiomic data.

I followed-up my talk with an open Q&A session after the course.
