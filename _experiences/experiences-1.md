"With spatially resolved transcriptomics, molecular biologists can visualise the morphology of gene expression of hundreds or thousands of genes in the sample tissue. This new technology has paved the way to new integration methods that combine spatially resolved transcriptomics data with single-cell data allowing for spatial resolution of other modalities as well as superior gene coverage. Here, I test cutting-edge methods to map single-cell RNA-sequencing data onto 10x Xenium spatial transcriptomics Neuroblastoma samples and compare their performance. I used the trained models to further map single-cell gene activity and transcription factor activity scores to obtain a multimodal spatially resolved dataset. I find Tangram and MaxFuse to perform outstandingly for mapping single-cell RNA sequencing with MaxFuse surpassing Tangram for the purposes of mapping transcription factor and gene activity. With the new integrated data, I characterise the clusters of cells with a spatially distinct arrangement to attempt to infer their role in the tumour microenvironment and show the benefits of multiple omics data types."
