# Classification of abnormal behaviour in D. melanogaster larvae

## Abstract

"Worldwide populations of arthropods are declining due to the synergy of anthropogenic effects such as climate change and intense pesticide usage. Whilst the latter is supposed to be limited to agricultural areas, small concentrations of pesticides have been measured in supposedly natural habitats mainly due to diffusion through the atmosphere.

In this project, we analyse a large screen of Drosophila melanogaster larvae’s behavioural data under the influence of various pesticides at different concentrations. We use different image analysis softwares that return categorical and continuous data to detect aberrations in the larvae’s behaviour. With a new analysis pipeline to detect significant aberrations, we identify a remarkable fraction (> 25 %) of pesticides that induce statistically significant aberrations in the larvae’s behaviour. Besides insecticides, herbicides and fungicides regularly induce aberrant behaviour, even at a concentration of 2 µM."
