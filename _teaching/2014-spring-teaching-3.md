In this position, I served as a tutor for 4 teams of 4 molecular biotechnology students each along their first data analysis project in R on the topic of DNA methylation in AML and ALL cancer data. I provided debugging assistance, data analysis mentorship, GitHub usage mentorship and aid to write a protocol to dcoument their entire analysis in R Markdown. All teams were then tasked to present their porject at the end of the course, and they each scored very well with grades from 1.0 to 1.3 on a scale from 1 (best) to 4 (worst).
